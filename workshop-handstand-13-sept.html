Before you book

Do I need anything?

No. This is the one workshop in the calendar with no prerequisite at all — no wall hold, no strength standard, no experience. If you can put your hands on the floor, you can come.

✓ Complete beginners

You have never taken weight on your hands. This workshop was designed around you, and there will be others in the room in exactly the same position. You will spend the session at the wall and you will leave having held a handstand.

✓ Already freestanding

You get the second half's individual work — line diagnosis, finger pressure, and correction strategy, on camera. You will be pushed, and grouped with people at your level rather than averaged into the middle.

One honest exception

If you are holding 20+ seconds freestanding and want one-arm work, a 90-minute group session is the wrong tool — ongoing coaching will serve you far better. Everyone else: come.
