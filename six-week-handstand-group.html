Good to know

Do I need to be in London?

No. Everything is online: the app, your clips, the calls and the group. If you are in London you are welcome at the Saturday class too.

How much time does it take?

Three sessions a week of 30 to 45 minutes, filming one clip, and the hour’s call if you can make it.

What if I miss a call?

It is recorded, and your clip still gets its reply that week.

What do I need?

A wall, a phone to film on, and the app on your phone or laptop. Your login comes with your place.

Do I have to join the WhatsApp group?

No. In WhatsApp your number is visible to the rest of the group, so if you would rather not, follow everything in the app and on the calls.

What if I have an injury?

Tell me when you book, and I will tell you honestly whether the group is right for you now.

How do I pay?

Card or Apple Pay through Stripe. You get a confirmation by email, and I will be in touch within 48 hours with your test and your login.

Not sure it is for you?

Book a free 15 minute call and I will tell you honestly where I would start you, even if the answer is the free app.
