Sound familiar?

You can't see what you're doing upside down.

I spent years repeating the same mistake because nobody was watching. One look from the side usually finds it.

  • The kick up never quite gets to the wall.
  • You're fine on the wall and lost the second you leave it.
  • You balance for two seconds, then fall the same way every time.
  • You've done the drills for months and nothing has moved.
How to film it

Thirty seconds of set up.

  • Side on. Not from the front, not from behind.
  • Whole body in frame. Hands and toes, the whole way up.
  • One clip, up to three minutes. A normal attempt, not your best ever.
  • Straight off your phone. No editing, no slow motion.
