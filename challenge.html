Questions
Do I need a handstand already?

No. Foundations starts on the floor and the wall. The quiz puts you where you are.

What about rest days?

Wednesday and Saturday are lighter, and the minimum is 5 minutes. A flagged day is never held against you.

Do I have to do thirty in a row?

No. The board shows the days you did, not a streak that breaks. Start late and join in.

What happens after?

The Ladder carries on from your stage, free on Foundations. Your day 30 film shows how far you came.
