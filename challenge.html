How it works

Practise, log it, get better.

1
Practise every day

Any practice counts: your own, a class, or the app's sessions. Ill, injured or away? Flag the day and it does not count against you.

2
Log it, open a door

Each day you log opens a door on your advent calendar. Everyone's are on the board, and one tap posts yours to the challenge group chat.

3
See how far you come

A two minute quiz finds your level. You film its test when you join and again at the end, try it on Sundays, and behind each door is something to work on for your level.

Questions
Do I need a handstand already?

No. Foundations starts on the floor and the wall. The quiz puts you where you are.

Do I have to use the app's sessions?

No. Practise however you like and log it. The app's sessions are there if you want a plan.

What about rest days?

Five minutes counts. Ill, injured or away? Flag the day and it is never held against you.

Do I have to do every day in a row?

No. The board shows the days you did, not a streak that breaks. Start late and join in.

Is there a group chat?

Yes, for everyone on the challenge. The link is in the app once you join.
