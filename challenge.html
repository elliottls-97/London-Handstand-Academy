A plan alongside it

Train it step by step.

The app breaks the handstand into six stages, from your first kick up to a freestanding hold and a press. Each stage has a session for your level every day, every drill filmed, and seven check points to climb before the next. Finish a session and it opens that day's door for you.

Questions

Before you join.

Is it really free?

Yes. Join, log your days and you are in for every prize. Training with the app and help from me are extras, and paying does not change anybody's chances.

Do I need a handstand already?

No. Foundations starts on the floor and the wall. The quiz puts you where you are.

What do I film?

Your honest best handstand on day 1, any skill you like, and again in the last week. Thirty seconds is plenty.

Do I have to use the app's sessions?

No. Practise however you like and log it. The app's sessions are there if you want a plan.

What about rest days?

Five minutes counts. Ill, injured or away? Flag the day and it is never held against you.

Do I have to do every day in a row?

No. The board shows the days you did, not a streak that breaks. Start late and join in.

Can I see how others are doing?

Yes. Everyone on the challenge is on the board in the app, first names only, and you can share your days wherever you like.

The rules

Simple, and fair.

  • Joining is your entry. It is free, and paying for help does not change anybody's chances.
  • Any practice counts. Open a door for each day you practise; a rest day is never held against you.
  • To win anything, open at least 20 of the 24 doors. Each day marked as a rest day takes one off that, down to 12.
  • Most improved, the parallettes and a 1-2-1: film your honest best on day 1, and again from Tuesday 22 to Sunday 27 December. I judge the improvement: a longer chest-to-wall hold, better freestanding balance, or how far you climb through the app. A start film that is not a real attempt can be left out. My pick is final.
  • The draw: four winners, picked at random from everyone who opened enough doors. The first gets three months of the app, the other three a free class in January at OverGravity, Shadwell (a form check from me if you cannot get to London).
  • Most app days: the two people who log the most days with the app's session (its gold days on the leaderboard). Most minutes in the app's sessions breaks a tie. The first gets three months of the app and a London Handstand Academy t-shirt, the second three months of the app.
  • One prize each. Winners shown in the app on Monday 28 December and told by email. Parallettes and the t-shirt go to a UK address. The 1-2-1 is in London or on a video call. No cash alternative.
  • The challenge is run by London Handstand Academy. Apple is not a sponsor and is not involved in it in any way.
