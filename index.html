Six stages, wrist prep through to the press. Each one has its own drills, its own levels, and check points you have to reach before the next opens.

A session in two taps

Say how long you have and how hard you want it. Fifteen minutes is a spread of six drills at one set each; forty five goes deeper on ten.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need to be advanced?

No, most clients start somewhere between "can kick up" and "can’t hold it yet." Complete beginners are welcome; the programme starts from wherever you are.

Can I do this online?

Yes. Programming and video form-checks work anywhere, you film your drills, I review and correct. In-person sessions at OverGravity are an optional add-on for London folk.

What if I’m injured or have dodgy wrists?

Tell me at the audit. We build around it, wrist prep and load management are baked into every programme, and Beth (Chartered Physiotherapist) is on the team.

What is Inner Circle?

The online programme with accountability and regular feedback on top. You get chased every week to finish the plan rather than left to drift, the plan is adjusted weekly if it needs it, any clip you send comes back with a voice over and a video reply, there is an optional 20 minute call each month going through your progress, and you have direct access in between. £320 a month, with a 60 minute session in London each month included, and no joining fee. There are three places, so it is application only: answer the questions and I will tell you honestly whether it fits.

Is there a joining fee?

No, on any plan. Your onboarding call, the video assessment and your first training block are all included in the first month. If you have taken an £80 one-off session in the last 14 days, that comes off your first month instead.

Do I have to train in person?

No. The programme works fully online. A session in London is an optional £70 a month on top, so it is £120 online or £190 with a session, and you can add or drop it any time. You are never quietly paying for a session you cannot get to. Want to meet more often than once a month? Tell me on the call and I will price it.

How do I start?

Pick your plan (£120/mo online, or £190/mo with a session in London) and book your onboarding call. Prefer to try first? A one-off 1-2-1 is £80, credited to your first month if you join within 14 days. Unsure? Book a free 15-min call. If you have questions before committing, book a free 15-minute onboarding call, we will talk through your goals and pick the right option, and there is no obligation to sign up.
