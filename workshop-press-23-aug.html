Who's teaching

This one is led by Tamzen Moulding — 20 years teaching hand balance, currently on the degree course at the National Centre for Circus Arts. Elliott coaches alongside her, so for a group this size you get two sets of eyes and twice the hands-on correction across the two hours.

Tamzen Moulding
@tamzenmoulding
Lead coach · National Centre for Circus Arts

An internationally renowned hand-balancer and teacher with 20 years of teaching experience, from complete beginners to working professionals. Tamzen teaches on the degree course at the National Centre for Circus Arts, alongside their recreational programme, and works as an acrobatic consultant on award-winning theatre shows. She has taught handstand and dance workshops around the world — retreats, festivals and outreach projects for adults and children.

Her teaching ethos: work with your body, not against it. Every body is built differently, so the job is to find the technique that works for yours. That does not mean ignoring weaknesses — it means you stop trying to force a shape that was never going to fit you.

Before you book

What you'll need

One thing, and you can test it today. The press builds directly on it — without it the day is spent on the base instead of the press.

✓ Chest-to-wall handstand · 30 seconds

Facing the wall, walked in close, body straight, hips stacked over your shoulders. Held for 30 seconds — pushing tall, not fighting to stay there.

Not there yet?

Build it first — the free Handstand Ladder app has the progression in order, or come to our beginner workshop. Then this one is worth your £50.

You should be able to hold a chest-to-wall handstand for 30 seconds. If you're close but not sure, message us before booking and we'll tell you honestly. The press needs that base to build on.

Some people do — usually the ones who had the strength all along and just needed the lean and timing fixed. Most don't get it on the day, and that's normal. Everyone leaves knowing their exact sticking point and how to train it.

We cover the press from the floor — both straddle and pike paths — and give you the drills for whichever suits your body. Compression, take-off and the unroll all get their own time.
