Falling is a skill
How to fall out of a handstand safelyFive steps that make falling out light, then automatic: bail outs, cartwheels, crawling out on the way to the wall, a partner spot, kick up bounces.
The way up
Chair, wall walks, then the wallThe order that gets a beginner to a chest to wall handstand: the chair assisted handstand, wall walks, learning to fall, then the wall.
