Film it

You cannot feel a bent hip. You can see one.

What feels straight almost never is. A hip that is bent, a back that is arched, shoulders that have not opened: you cannot feel any of them from inside the handstand, and you can see all of them in a thirty second clip.

How to film it

  1. Phone on the floor, side onLean it against something low, a few steps away.
  2. Your whole body in frameHands to toes, with some floor and wall around you.
  3. One attempt, then watch itWatch for the line from wrists to toes. Where does it bend?

What to look for

One straight line from wrists to toes. The usual breaks are at the shoulders (not pushed tall), the ribs (flared, which arches the back) and the hips (piked). Pick the one furthest from the floor and fix that first; the ones below it often follow.

Then log it

Each stage in the app has check points: the things you have to be able to do before the next stage opens. Log them as you go, with the clip, and if you are being coached send it for a form check and get a video reply.
