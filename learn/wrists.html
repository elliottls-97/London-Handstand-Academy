Wrists first

Two minutes before every session.

Most handstand pain in the first month is wrists, and most of that is skipped warm ups. Your wrists are about to hold your whole body weight at ninety degrees, which they do not do at any other point in your day. Two minutes gets them ready.

1Circles, both ways
2Flexion and extension, the other hand helping
3Weight shifts on all fours
2mTwo minutes, every session

Circles

Palm lifts and fingertip lifts

In table top, lift the heel of each hand while the fingers stay down, then the fingers while the heel stays. Slowly. This is the range a handstand lives in.

Weight shifts

Shift your weight over one hand, tap the other, and back. The wrist learns to take load at an angle, which is what balancing on it will ask for.

The days you are not up to it

There is a mobility day in the app: wrists and shoulders, ten minutes, no stage work. It still counts, and it is the day that keeps a habit alive. The app puts the warm up at the top of every session for the same reason.
