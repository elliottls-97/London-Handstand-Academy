Off the wall

Why can’t I balance off the wall yet?

You can hold your shape on the wall now, so why not just kick up in the middle of the room? I know it is tempting.

What each stage teaches

Wall Work teaches you what a line feels like, with the wall to get you there. Pushing More teaches you to take yourself a little off the wall while keeping that position. The shrug, the scapular elevation, is usually not quite there yet, and this is the stage that gets it there.

These are the drills that do it:

1Scapula Shrugs
2Slide Away
3Tuck Slides
4Single Leg Wall Removals

Lying on the wall is not the same

People come to me saying they have worked on handstands for ages and can get their legs off the wall. Most of the time the chest is sunk in and they are lying on the wall. This stage is about getting off it properly, so that when the balance work comes in stages four and five, the ability is already underneath it.

You still kick up

The ladder is a step by step route away from the wall, and the entries level up alongside it, so you keep doing the fun stuff and kicking up into handstands. The difference is getting in and out of the handstand consistently, rather than kicking up and hoping to balance.
