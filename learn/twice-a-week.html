How often

Two sessions a week moves a handstand. One keeps it.

Nobody needs an hour. A fifteen minute session is six drills at one set each, and two of those a week beats one heroic Sunday every time. The handstand is a skill before it is a strength, and skills are built by showing up often, not by showing up long.

Why two

One session a week holds what you have. Two is where things move, because the second session lands while the first is still in your body. Three is better again, if your wrists agree. Beyond that, most people are better off resting.

Why short

Handstand practice is high quality or it is nothing. The first six drills, done well, teach more than the next six done tired. Fifteen minutes stops before the form goes.

How the app builds it

Say how long you have and it builds a session to fit. It starts further down your stage each time, so Tuesday and Thursday are different workouts, not the same one again. Easier, as written or harder, and it asks how it felt at the end so the next one fits.
