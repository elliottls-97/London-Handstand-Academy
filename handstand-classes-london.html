    Good to know

    Do I need to be able to do a handstand already?

    No. Complete beginners start at the wall and learn to kick up and come down safely. The class is grouped by level, from never been upside down to press and one arm work.

    What should I wear and bring?

    Normal training kit you can move in, and a water bottle. Bare feet or socks, no shoes on the mats. Bring your phone if you want your before and after clips.

    How big are the groups?

    Small, usually six to twelve people, with two coaches, so you get seen and corrected.

    How do I pay?

    Card or Apple Pay through Stripe when you book, on this site. No account and no login. You get a confirmation straight away and a reminder the day before, by email.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Up to 24 hours before, cancel and you are refunded, or move to another date for free. The link is in your confirmation email and your reminder. Inside 24 hours the place cannot be refilled, so it is not refunded.

    Can I learn handstands online instead?

    Yes. Online coaching starts from footage of how you train now, with your programme in the app and a video reply every two weeks showing what to change. There is also a free app, the Handstand Ladder, to start on your own.

    Not sure a class is right?

    The Handstand Ladder app has a free first stage with the drills we teach, filmed, and a one minute quiz that tells you where you are. Try it free. Or book a free 15 minute call and Elliott will tell you honestly where to start.
