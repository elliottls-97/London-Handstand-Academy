What a class is

Small groups, usually six to twelve people, so you are watched and corrected rather than following along at the back. Every drill has an easier and a harder version. Beginners learn to kick up to the wall and come down safely; the freestanding group works on balance and line; the advanced group works on shapes, presses and one arm foundations.
