Angie — where to go from here

Three ways in, and the honest version of what each one costs. Nothing here expires, and nothing needs deciding today.

The short version

You want in-person in the mix. That changes which option makes sense.

The difference between the tiers is not how much you get. It is how much of the work of staying on track is yours. Coaching gives you the right programme, form checks and a person to ask. Inner Circle adds someone chasing you weekly. In-person puts hands on it.

You want hands-on sessions and you want them regularly, so the sensible starting point is coaching with in-person built into the month, then adding sessions on top when you want them.

In-person is always an add-on rather than a package. That is deliberate: it means you can have as much or as little of it as you want in any given month, and stop without losing the programme underneath it.
