Between sessions

What the programme actually is

Not a PDF. It is an app you open on the day, and it tells you what to do and times it for you.

  • Your own page, rebuilt every six weeks. Written for your body, your week and what the last block showed — not a template with your name on it.
  • A demo video and cues on every single drill. You never have to remember what I meant.
  • A guided follow-along. Press start and it runs the session — every set and every rest timed, so you are training rather than clock-watching.
  • Or work down the list at your own pace, ticking drills off and logging what you actually did.
  • Too easy or too hard on any drill, one tap. It comes to me and it changes the next block.
  • Your holds, reps and check points tracked, so progress is a number you can see rather than a feeling about whether it is working.
  • Everything works offline. Gym wifi is not a reason to lose a session.
It installs to your phone like an app. No app store, no faff — you open a link once and it is on your home screen.
What this means in practice: you leave a session, and the work we did is in your hand the next morning with the reasoning attached. That is the part that makes fortnightly or monthly sessions work — you are not trying to remember a correction from three weeks ago.
