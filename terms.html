Before starting, you must read and accept the participation waiver and health declaration, and tell us honestly about injuries, conditions, pregnancy, medication and anything else that could affect your safety.

We are handstand coaches. We are not doctors, physiotherapists or other healthcare professionals, and nothing we give you is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you have a condition or an injury, get clearance from a qualified healthcare professional before you start. If something hurts, stop, and speak to a professional.
