How coaching works

Inside a real programme.

A real client’s coaching app, exactly as they see it, with their name taken off.

01
The questions

You tell me where you are.

Your goal, where you are today, anything that hurts, and how many hours and days you can really train. Up to three clips. Then a free 15 minute call, before anything is paid.

02
Your programme

Built around how much you can train.

This client trains five days a week: a 60 minute main session two or three times, a shorter press day, a lighter balance day for any day. Every drill filmed, with a note from me.

03
Check points

Targets set for you, signed off by me.

What you should be able to do by the end of the block. Tick one when you can, film it, and I sign it off or tell you what’s missing.

04
Video replies

Film it. Get a video back.

Send a clip from the app. I reply on video, with lines drawn on your body and the one thing to change. It lands on your home screen.

05
Bad habits

Spotted before they set in.

The things you can’t feel are the ones that stick. A line on the clip shows you exactly where it goes, before months of practice bake it in.

06
The chat

Talk to me in the app.

Questions between sessions go in the chat, next to your clips and your programme. In their third week I spotted this client’s legs separating on tuck slides. They couldn’t feel it. On the video, they could see it.

07
Your next block

A plan that moves with you.

Every block is written from what you sent in the last one: new check points, a lighter day for travel weeks, new work once we’ve tried it in person.

Before you ask.

Do I need to be advanced?

No. Most people start somewhere between “can kick up” and “can’t hold it yet”. Complete beginners are welcome.

Is every session live with you?

No. You train on your own, film it, and I review. Live time is a London session or weekly coaching.

What if I’m injured or have dodgy wrists?

Tell me on the call and we build around it. Wrist prep is in every programme, and Beth, a chartered physiotherapist, is on the team.
