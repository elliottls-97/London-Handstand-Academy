Questions

Before you start.

Can I join if I still use the wall?

Yes. Most people start somewhere between “can kick up” and “can’t hold it yet”. Complete beginners are welcome, and your programme starts on the stage you are on.

Does it work online?

Yes. The programme and the form checks work anywhere: you film, I review. Sessions at OverGravity in London are an optional extra.

Is every session live with you?

No. You train on your own, following your programme in the app, in your own time. The coaching is my eye on your clips and the plan changing with them.

What if I’m injured or have dodgy wrists?

Tell me on the call and we build around it. Wrist prep is in every programme, and Beth, a Chartered Physiotherapist at Flexibility Matters, is on the team.

Is there a joining fee?

No, on any plan.

How do I start?

Send me a clip: your first video reply is free. Or apply: two minutes and up to three clips of you now, then a free 15 minute consultation where we agree the plan before anything is paid.
