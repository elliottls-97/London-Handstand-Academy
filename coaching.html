Questions

Before you ask.

Can I join if I still use the wall?

Yes. Most people start somewhere between “can kick up” and “can’t hold it yet”. Complete beginners are welcome.

Does it work online?

Yes. The programme and the form checks work anywhere: you film, I review. Sessions at OverGravity in London are an optional extra, set up on the call.

Is every session live with you?

No. You train in your own time, following your programme in the app. The coaching is my eye on your clips and the plan changing with them.

What if I’m injured or have dodgy wrists?

Tell me on the call and we build around it. Wrist prep is in every programme, and Beth, a Chartered Physiotherapist at Flexibility Matters, is on the team.
