Questions people ask.

Do I need to be able to do a handstand already?

No. Most people start somewhere between "can kick up" and "cannot hold it", and complete beginners are welcome. The programme starts from wherever your first film shows you are.

Can I do it fully online?

Yes. The programme, the films and the video feedback all work anywhere. London sessions are an extra for people who are nearby.

What happens after I pay?

You answer a short questionnaire and send a clip of where you are now. Your first block is written from them, usually within a few days, and appears in the app. On weekly coaching there is a video call first.

Do I need a gym?

No. Almost everything is a floor, a wall and a chair. If you have a gym, some days use a box.

Can I stop?

It is a month at a time. Cancel from your account and it runs to the end of the month you have paid for.

What if I have an injury?

Say so at the start. The programme is built around it, and Beth Shum, a Chartered Physiotherapist, is on the team.
